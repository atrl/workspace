# 2023 Regime Shift Analysis

## Date
2026-04-26

## Goal
Understand why 2023-2024 performance (wf_2024: +29.8%, MaxDD 32.1%) lags 2020 performance (+106.7%, MaxDD 22.4%), and identify improvement levers.

## Canonical Baseline
balanced_rule_residual_v19 with min_cap=0.20

## Key Data Points

| Metric | wf_2020 | wf_2024 | Delta |
|--------|---------|---------|-------|
| Return | +106.7% | +29.8% | -76.9pp |
| MaxDD | 22.4% | 32.1% | +9.7pp |
| Sharpe | 1.95 | 0.71 | -1.24 |
| Relative vs BnH | +46.9pp | +32.4pp | -14.5pp |

Both windows outperform BnH significantly, but 2024 is weaker on all metrics.

## Hypotheses

1. **Regime ambiguity**: 2023-2024 has more mixed regime signals (regime_risk hovers around 0.5-0.6) compared to 2020's clear crisis/recovery
2. **False signals**: Actor may increase position during temporary dips that become sustained declines
3. **Recovery timing**: 2020 had a sharp V-shaped recovery; 2023-2024 had a slower, choppier recovery
4. **Feature drift**: Some features may have lost predictive power in 2023-2024 regime

## Evidence

### 2020 vs 2024 Comparison

| Metric | 2020 | 2024 |
|--------|------|------|
| Return | +106.7% | +24.9% |
| MaxDD | 22.4% | 32.1% |
| Avg position | 0.538 | 0.473 |
| Avg regime_risk | 0.544 | 0.545 |
| Regime_risk std | **0.191** | **0.161** |
| Ambiguous regime days | 24.1% | **27.0%** |
| Loss > 3% days | 19 | **27** |
| Avg position on loss days | 0.629 | 0.556 |

### Key Finding 1: 2024 is fundamentally more unstable
- 27 days with >3% loss vs 19 in 2020
- More ambiguous regime days (27% vs 24%)
- Lower regime_risk std (0.161 vs 0.191) = slower regime transitions

### Key Finding 2: False safety signals
Worst 2024 days show regime_risk giving false "safe" readings:
- 2024-01-26: ret=-6.96%, pos=0.818, regime=0.397 (normal!)
- 2024-06-24: ret=-6.77%, pos=0.776, regime=0.506 (ambiguous)
- 2024-03-08: ret=-8.36%, pos=0.650, regime=0.534 (ambiguous)

In contrast, 2020 worst days had regime_risk > 0.60 (correctly identifying stress):
- 2020-09-03: ret=-12.39%, pos=0.681, regime=0.609
- 2020-06-11: ret=-11.02%, pos=0.681, regime=0.645

### Root Cause
**Regime detection fails to predict sudden crashes in "normal" regimes.**

When regime_risk < 0.50, position cap = 1.0 (no restriction). Actor can hold 80%+ position.
If market crashes suddenly while regime_risk is still low, losses are catastrophic.

This is a fundamental limitation of regime-based risk management.

## Conclusion

2024's weaker performance is not due to a single fixable parameter.
It is a structural challenge: sudden crashes in "normal" regimes.

**No quick fix available.** Potential long-term directions:
1. Add hard stop-loss (e.g., cut position by 50% after -5% single-day loss)
2. Use shorter-window features for faster regime detection
3. Add volatility-adjusted position sizing independent of regime
