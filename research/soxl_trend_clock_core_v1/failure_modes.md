# Failure modes

- `outer contamination`: 2023-2026 SOXL evidence was viewed during architecture refinement.
- `cross-asset failure`: the frozen 140/150/160 and 8% rule lagged TQQQ, UPRO, SPXL, and TECL buy-and-hold in their reused outer windows.
- `tail remains material`: SOXL drawdown is reduced, not eliminated; a roughly 65% drawdown remains possible.
- `regime dependence`: most relative value comes from avoiding leveraged decay in selected semiconductor drawdowns.
- `execution`: qlib reports and the explicit open-to-open replay can differ because qlib models cash, exchange fills, and portfolio accounting. Both are retained in the recorder.
