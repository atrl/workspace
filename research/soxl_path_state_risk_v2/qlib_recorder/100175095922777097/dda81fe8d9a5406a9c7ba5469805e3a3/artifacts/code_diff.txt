-# SOXL Path-State Risk V2 Design
-
-## Decision
-
-Build a qlib-native, factor-only research card that represents SOXL risk as
-state interactions with separate forecast horizons. Do not train a predictive
-model and do not average the new features into one generic pressure score.
-
-The card tests whether two transparent states add stable future-risk evidence
-beyond the existing price-only baseline:
-
-1. a five-session `fast_turbulence_risk` state while the trend is still up;
-2. a twenty-session `structural_chop_risk` state after high-level, high-volatility
-   chop persists.
-
-The first state is a short-horizon volatility-control input, not a directional
-sell signal. The second state remains diagnostic unless its rare-event and
-episode gates pass independently.
-
-## Evidence behind the design
-
-The qlib SOXL/SOXX daily provider contains 3,897 aligned sessions from
-2011-01-03 through 2026-07-02 with no required-field nulls or duplicate dates.
-
-A descriptive-only 20% reversal segmentation found 65 upswings and 64
-downswings. Median upswings lasted 21 sessions, returned 54.8%, and had 74.3%
-annualized realized volatility. Median downswings lasted 12 sessions, returned
--31.3%, and had 102.0% annualized realized volatility. This segmentation uses
-future pivots and therefore must never become a feature or label input.
-
-The causal state analysis showed the useful asymmetry:
-
-- stable uptrends had a 12.7% development and 19.3% outer five-session
-  10%-drawdown rate;
-- turbulent uptrends had a 32.8% development and 33.3% outer five-session
-  10%-drawdown rate;
-- turbulent uptrends also retained substantial upside, including a 69.4%
-  outer probability of gaining at least 10% within five sessions;
-- persistent high-level chop had a 24.0% development and 87.5% outer
-  twenty-session 20%-drawdown rate, but the outer sample contained only 16
-  daily observations and must remain rare-event evidence.
-
-Single continuous candidates were unstable. Leverage-residual pressure was
-stronger in development than outer data, while intraday selling pressure had
-the opposite pattern. The existing price-only composite remained the more
-stable outer baseline. V2 therefore tests logical state interactions and state
-duration rather than adding another equal-weight continuous family.
-
-## Fixed scope
-
-- Card: `research/soxl_path_state_risk_v2/`.
-- Instruments: SOXL and SOXX for aligned source evidence; SOXL is the evaluated
-  security.
-- Primary frequency: daily.
-- Signal time: after close `t`; any later portfolio experiment must first apply
-  the target at open `t+1`.
-- Method: transparent factor-only diagnostics, no fitted model and no fitted
-  factor weights.
-- Data cutoff: 2026-07-02. The card must not describe this as a live
-  2026-07-20 state.
-- Phase 2 only: use the complete SOXL/SOXX 15-minute provider to refine timing
-  after the daily state passes. It is excluded from V2 selection.
-
-## Non-goals
-
-- Do not tune another LGBM, XGBoost, logistic, or neural model.
-- Do not use future-aware swing pivots as inputs.
-- Do not use `turnover / volume` or turnover-derived VWAP until the adjusted
-  price and turnover basis is repaired and audited.
-- Do not use short external bellwether intraday history, current-holdings-
-  backfilled constituents, options, order book, or trade-direction proxies.
-- Do not map a negative trend directly to maximum risk. Completed downside
-  states have high rebound probability and require a separate recovery study.
-- Do not create a production position rule in this card.

-## Base state variables
-
-All state variables use observations available at close `t`. Rolling
-normalization includes the current completed session but never a future row.
-
-### Signed path efficiency
-
-```text
-signed_efficiency_20(t)
-  = log(close_t / close_t-20)
-    / sum(abs(log_return_i), i=t-19..t)
-```
-
-It lies approximately in `[-1, 1]`. Positive values identify efficient upward
-paths, negative values identify efficient downward paths, and values near zero
-identify directionless movement. Unlike the previous absolute efficiency
-pressure, it preserves direction.
-
-### Volatility rank
-
-```text
-rv_20(t) = std(log_return, 20) * sqrt(252)
-rv_rank_20_252(t) = historical percentile of rv_20(t)
-```
-
-The percentile uses at most the trailing 252 completed observations and needs
-at least 60 observations.
-
-### High-level position
-
-```text
-range_position_252(t)
-  = (close_t - rolling_min_252)
-    / (rolling_max_252 - rolling_min_252)
-```
-
-The implementation must handle a zero denominator as missing rather than
-silently filling a value.
-
-## Feature design
-
-### Trend permission
-
-`trend_permission` is positive evidence for maintaining ordinary trend
-participation. It is not inverted and presented as generic risk.
-
-```text
-trend_up_strength
-  = clip((signed_efficiency_20 - 0.15) / 0.20, 0, 1)
-
-ordinary_vol_permission
-  = clip((0.75 - rv_rank_20_252) / 0.20, 0, 1)
-
-trend_permission
-  = trend_up_strength * ordinary_vol_permission
-```
-
-### Fast turbulence risk
-
-```text
-fast_vol_stress
-  = clip((rv_rank_20_252 - 0.67) / 0.13, 0, 1)
-
-fast_turbulence_risk
-  = trend_up_strength * fast_vol_stress
-```
-
-The product is intentional. A high volatility rank without an uptrend, or an
-uptrend without elevated volatility, cannot produce maximum fast turbulence
-risk. The interpretable event audit uses
-`signed_efficiency_20 >= 0.25 and rv_rank_20_252 >= 0.75`; the continuous score
-is the factor used for diagnostics.
-
-### Structural chop risk
-
-Define the causal state flag:
-
-```text
-structural_chop_state
-  = range_position_252 >= 0.70
-    and abs(signed_efficiency_20) <= 0.20
-    and rv_rank_20_252 >= 0.67
-```
-
-`state_age` is the number of consecutive completed sessions for which this
-exact flag remains true. It resets to zero immediately after any condition
-fails.
-
-```text
-high_level_gate
-  = clip((range_position_252 - 0.60) / 0.10, 0, 1)
-
-chop_gate
-  = clip((0.25 - abs(signed_efficiency_20)) / 0.10, 0, 1)
-
-structural_vol_stress
-  = clip((rv_rank_20_252 - 0.60) / 0.15, 0, 1)
-
-persistence_score
-  = clip(state_age / 4, 0, 1)
-
-structural_chop_risk
-  = high_level_gate
-    * chop_gate
-    * structural_vol_stress
-    * persistence_score
-```
-
-This differs from generic high-level duration: duration contributes only after
-level, directionlessness, and volatility conditions are simultaneously true.
-
-## Horizon-specific labels
-
-Both labels start strictly after close `t`.
-
-### Five-session fast-tail label
-
-```text
-fast_tail_5d = 1 when:
-  minimum(close_t+1..close_t+5) / close_t - 1 <= -10%
-  and close_t+5 / close_t - 1 <= -5%
-```
-
-Also retain five-session maximum favorable excursion and a `>= +10%` upside
-indicator. Fast turbulence cannot pass if it reduces downside evidence only by
-selecting states that eliminate the observed right tail.
-
-### Twenty-session structural-break label
-
-```text
-structural_break_20d = 1 when:
-  minimum(close_t+1..close_t+20) / close_t - 1 <= -20%
-  and close_t+20 / close_t - 1 <= -10%
-```
-
-Also retain twenty-session maximum favorable excursion and a `>= +20%` upside
-indicator.
-
-The final 21 observations of every segment must be purged so neither label nor
-path statistic crosses a segment boundary.

-## Segments and selection discipline
-
-- Development: 2011-01-03 through 2022-12-31.
-- Validation: 2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31.
-- Frozen test: 2025-01-01 through 2026-07-02.
-- Annual and fixed stress-window reports are required.
-- No threshold, direction, horizon, or feature formula may be changed after
-  reading validation or test results.
-- Consecutive flagged days are grouped into episodes for support and stability
-  gates; daily observations and episode counts are both reported.
-
-## Diagnostics and incremental evidence
-
-Evaluate `fast_turbulence_risk` only against `fast_tail_5d` and evaluate
-`structural_chop_risk` only against `structural_break_20d`.
-
-For each feature and segment report:
-
-- daily observations, positive labels, episodes, and positive episodes;
-- AUC, average-precision lift, Q5-Q1 event-rate lift, and quintile
-  monotonicity;
-- conditional event-rate lift inside fixed price-only quintiles;
-- mean terminal return, maximum adverse excursion, maximum favorable
-  excursion, and downside/upside hit rates;
-- yearly direction and threshold-neighborhood stability.
-
-The benchmark is the existing equal-weight `price_only` score built from range
-position, low absolute trend efficiency, sign-change density, and realized
-volatility rank. New features must show positive conditional lift after fixing
-the benchmark quintile; a favorable standalone AUC is insufficient.
-
-## Evidence gates
-
-`fast_turbulence_risk` is retained only when:
-
-- development has at least 50 daily observations and 20 episodes;
-- validation and test each have at least 10 daily observations and three
-  episodes;
-- event-rate lift, AUC direction, and Q5-Q1 direction are positive in
-  development, validation, and test;
-- conditional event-rate lift inside price-only quintiles is positive in
-  validation and test;
-- the five-session upside diagnostics remain explicit so a later controller
-  cannot interpret the feature as an automatic cash signal.
-
-`structural_chop_risk` is retained as validated evidence only when:
-
-- development has at least 50 daily observations and 15 episodes;
-- validation and test each have at least 10 daily observations and three
-  episodes;
-- all directional and conditional gates above pass for the twenty-session
-  label.
-
-If the structural test sample stays below 10 rows or three episodes, its status
-is `diagnostic_rare_event_support`; it cannot be promoted or used to relax any
-other gate.
-
-## Failure handling
-
-- If a feature changes direction across validation and test, record
-  `not_retained_direction_instability`.
-- If a label or feature uses a future row, record `blocked_temporal_leakage`.
-- If state-age episodes cross a segment boundary, split their evaluation
-  episode identifiers at the boundary. Preserve the causal state age computed
-  from earlier completed sessions because that history was observable at the
-  later segment's first date.
-- If data ends before the stated as-of date, record `needs_data`; do not call
-  the latest row current.
-- If the baseline cannot be reproduced within numerical tolerance, stop before
-  evaluating the new features.
