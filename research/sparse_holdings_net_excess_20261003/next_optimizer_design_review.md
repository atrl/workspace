# Preferred next design: feasible transfer plus36-variable COBYLA

This is a design review only. No new fit, source edit or market evaluation was performed. It supersedes the bespoke support-boundary/SQP option as the preferred first experiment because the standard method has a smaller implementation and validation surface.

The proposed policy keeps the verified parent's eight-unit encoder fixed. Subtract the complete SOXX score row from all four score rows; sparsemax is translation invariant, so this sets the SOXX row to zero without changing the mathematical policy. Optimize the other three rows over eight hidden activations, one bias and three actual holdings: `3*(8+1+3)=36` parameters. Parent and exact SOXX are independent incumbents. The current exact HOLD, sparsemax forward, two physical fee books, F and four raw gates remain unchanged.

COBYLA makes finite parameter proposals and builds local linear objective/constraint approximations from their measured outcomes. It does not require the sparsemax gradient to activate a currently inactive expert. Thus it can test directions inaccessible to the recorded zero Jacobian, including leaving seed71's economically redundant SOXX/HOLD support. `rhobeg=1` is naturally expressed in the simplex's score unit, but does not guarantee escape from every flat region or successful optimization. Warm-starting from an already feasible signal model reduces dependence on simultaneous encoder discovery and feasibility repair. It also changes initialization and capacity, so this is a bundled warm-start/frozen-encoder/optimizer hypothesis, not an isolated causal comparison of gradient methods.

Official SciPy documentation states that `maxiter` limits function evaluations, `rhobeg` sets initial variable changes, and `catol` is a numerical constraint-violation tolerance. The method uses PRIMA in current SciPy. None of those settings replaces the existing financial gates. Every evaluated36-vector should have a single cached exact path evaluation supplying both objective and all constraints; repeated SciPy callbacks at identical parameter bytes must not multiply hidden work or record fictitious distinct trials. Keep solver status, termination reason, `nfev`, actual unique parameter evaluations and incumbent selection distinct.

The objective can remain `-min(A_10,A_25)`; no derivative or smooth surrogate is required. Constraint values must correspond to the four current DD/CVaR gaps. An optimizer's constraint normalization may improve conditioning, but acceptance is always recomputed from unnormalized percentage-point gaps and the original1e-10pp tolerance. A final `success` flag is insufficient evidence of feasibility; a maximum-evaluation termination does not invalidate a previously measured feasible incumbent. Numerical invalidity still must be recorded explicitly, not recast as a scientific null.

Proposed engineering cap for registration is2000 objective evaluations per seed, with no restart or schedule grid. This number is only a bounded proposal, not a tuned result or an active setting. At the existing measured0.148s/two-cost N3000 forward, that is conservatively about5minutes per fit and about2hours for75fits on3workers, before solver overhead. Freeze/caching of hidden activations can reduce work; any faster evaluator must independently reproduce all targets, q, owned holdings, fees and multipliers from the current reference. Do not adjust the evaluation cap based on attained F. Profile the preregistered synthetic case first.

The synthetic gate must prove an incremental optimizer contribution. For each seed, require retained `F > embedded_parent_F + 1e-8` and all four unchanged raw risk gates. For the three-seed ensemble, compare its shared-own-book F against the shared-own-book ensemble of embedded parents, not the average of private-book F values. Inheriting a positive parent is not a passed optimizer test. If no improvement is found, retain the verified parent but mark this incremental gate failed. The fixed representability witness remains a witness, never a chosen initializer. If prior synthetic parent tensors were not retained, their exact Phase7 reproduction must be registered and saved separately; do not label reconstructed tensors as original artifacts.

Before any market follow-up, independently check exact embedding/gauge parity on the original mature rows, pin parent checkpoint/encoder/scaler identities, and verify parent feasibility using the corrected kernel. Do not choose historical parents by OOS outcomes. Missing outer-family parents may be reproduced only under their original protocol and cutoff after the new selection policy permits them; they are not permission to alter outer selection. A previous financially failed parent remains financially failed after a mathematically equivalent reparameterization.

The model class is still nonsmooth and nonconvex. COBYLA's finite proposals provide a real way to test inactive supports, not a convergence guarantee. The failed Phase8 control, original26ed versus retention3c690 provenance, and lack of a qualified deployment candidate remain unchanged.

Reference: [SciPy COBYLA options](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/optimize.minimize-cobyla.html), checked2026-10-03. Sparsemax translation invariance follows its Euclidean simplex projection definition in [Martins and Astudillo2016](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/martins16.html).


Before any registration, implementation or fitting of this proposal, review identified three further exact affine redundancies because hL+hS+hC=1. The proposed follow-up removes them by setting the SOXX holdings column tozero, absorbing its coefficient into the bias and subtracting it from the other two holdings coefficients. This reduces36 search coordinates to33 without changing the represented function class on normalized holdings. Constant-input cases retain further data-specific redundancy. No outcome or new fit selected this algebraic revision; the follow-up registration will define the final method.
